About CivicSync

CivicSync is a public coordination layer for municipal public works where departments plan and negotiate in the open, officials are verified through a chain of approval, and citizens can see exactly who is doing what, when, why, and what went wrong.

  • Official information requires Municipal Chairman approval before it is published.
  • Department employees are identity-verified internally but never named publicly — posts carry ✅ Verified Official — [Department].
  • Citizens are phone-verified; their numbers are encrypted and never displayed.
  • The Coordination Hub makes inter-department scheduling public, with automatic conflict detection within 200 m and overlapping dates.
  • Every delay requires a published reason; every action is audit-logged.
